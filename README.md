## ✨ Features

All included in VIPCore. [Modules](#-modules) add the perks.

- 👑 **VIP groups** — weighted groups, each with its own features and settings
- ⏳ **Timed or permanent grants** — on one server or all of them, extended or ended at will
- 🎛️ **VIP menu** — players switch perks on and off and pick their choices, `!vip`
- 🛠️ **Admin pages** — browse VIPs, extend, make permanent, remove and grant, in game
- ⌨️ **Admin commands** — grant by SteamID from chat, the console or RCON
- 🔔 **Welcome and expiry messages** — VIPs see their group and the time left
- 🗄️ **MySQL, MariaDB, PostgreSQL or SQLite** — one database for many servers
- 🍪 **Saved choices** — each player's picks kept with Cookies
- 🧩 **Module API** — other plugins add features, choices and pages, and grant VIP
- 🌍 **Translations** — English, Lithuanian and Brazilian Portuguese
